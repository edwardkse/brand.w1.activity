Activity - Brand Audit

In your assigned group, analyse a Malaysian brand and evaluate how it operates as a brand—not just a product.

1

Gather Real Evidence

  • Official website
  • Social media (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
  • Advertisements or campaigns
  • News articles or public perception
⚠️ No assumptions allowed! Ground every point in real observation.
2

Discuss All 5 Questions

  • Go beyond description (don’t just state what it does)
  • Explain why it matters & how it affects consumers
  • Provide concrete examples wherever possible
3

Presentation

  • Brief introduction of your assigned brand
  • Key insights from your group discussion
4

Peer Engagement

  • Listen actively while other groups present
  • Ask at least one critical question to another group
